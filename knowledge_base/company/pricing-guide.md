# Corvex Cloud — Official Pricing Guide

*Last updated: version applies to all new and renewing subscriptions. Pricing below reflects standard list pricing in USD; enterprise agreements may include negotiated terms.*

---

## 1. Overview

Corvex Cloud is offered across three subscription plans — **Starter**, **Professional**, and **Enterprise** — priced on a per-agent (per-seat) basis. Each plan is available on monthly or annual billing, with annual billing offering a meaningful discount over month-to-month rates. This guide outlines pricing, plan inclusions, and the policies governing upgrades, downgrades, billing, and cancellation.

All prices are per agent seat, per month, billed according to the selected billing frequency. A "seat" refers to any named user with login access to the Corvex Cloud agent workspace (customers/end users interacting with support are not seat-counted).

## 3. Starter Plan

### Monthly Price
**$29 per agent, per month**, billed monthly.

### Annual Price
**$24 per agent, per month**, billed annually as a single charge of $288 per seat per year — an effective savings of approximately 17% versus monthly billing.

### Included Features
- Shared inbox for email and web chat
- Basic case/ticket management
- Canned responses and simple macros
- Standard reporting dashboard (30-day lookback)
- Customer-facing help center (single language, single brand)
- Basic satisfaction (CSAT) surveys

### User Limits
- Minimum 3 seats, maximum 25 seats
- Teams exceeding 25 seats are automatically directed to Professional at renewal

### Storage Limits
- 25 GB pooled storage for attachments, files, and case history per account
- Case history retained for 18 months

### API Access
- Read-only API access for reporting and case export
- Rate limit: 60 requests/minute
- No webhook support at this tier

### Support Level
- Email support with a target first response within 1 business day
- Access to the Corvex Help Center and community forum
- No dedicated account contact

### Integrations
- Native integrations with common email providers and one web chat widget
- Zapier-based connections to a limited set of third-party apps
- No native CRM or e-commerce platform integrations

### Security Features
- Standard encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
- Role-based access with two permission levels (Admin, Agent)
- Standard password policy enforcement

### Upgrade Rules
- Starter accounts may upgrade to Professional or Enterprise at any time
- Upgrades take effect immediately upon confirmation
- Mid-cycle upgrades are prorated: the customer is charged the difference for the remainder of the current billing period

### Downgrade Rules
- Starter is the entry-level plan; there is no lower tier to downgrade to
- Accounts wishing to reduce seat count may do so at the next renewal date (see Section 8)

### Billing Frequency
- Monthly or annual, selected at signup
- Monthly plans are billed on the calendar date of initial signup each month
- Annual plans are billed once per year on the signup anniversary date

### Cancellation Terms
- Cancel anytime from account settings; no cancellation fee
- Monthly plans: cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid month
- Annual plans: cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid annual term; no partial-year refunds are issued except as required by applicable law

## 4. Professional Plan

### Monthly Price
**$69 per agent, per month**, billed monthly.

### Annual Price
**$55 per agent, per month**, billed annually as a single charge of $660 per seat per year — an effective savings of approximately 20% versus monthly billing.

### Included Features
- Everything in Starter, plus:
- Advanced routing and automation rules
- Multi-channel support (email, chat, social messaging, and voice add-on eligible)
- Custom workflows and automated triggers
- Advanced reporting with custom date ranges and exportable dashboards
- Multi-language help center (up to 5 languages)
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT) and Net Promoter Score (NPS) surveys
- Team-level performance analytics

### User Limits
- Minimum 5 seats, no fixed maximum (accounts above 150 seats are reviewed for Enterprise fit)

### Storage Limits
- 250 GB pooled storage for attachments, files, and case history per account
- Case history retained for 36 months
- Additional storage available as a metered add-on

### API Access
- Full read/write API access
- Rate limit: 300 requests/minute
- Webhook support for real-time event notifications

### Support Level
- Priority email and chat support with a target first response within 4 business hours
- Access to a named Customer Success contact (pooled, not 1:1 dedicated)
- Quarterly account health check-ins

### Integrations
- Native integrations with major CRM platforms, e-commerce platforms, and collaboration tools
- Full Zapier and native webhook support
- Access to the Corvex integration marketplace

### Security Features
- Everything in Starter, plus:
- Custom role-based permissions (beyond Admin/Agent)
- Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML 2.0
- Audit log access (90-day retention)
- IP allowlisting available

### Upgrade Rules
- Professional accounts may upgrade to Enterprise at any time
- Upgrades take effect immediately; billing is prorated for the remainder of the current cycle
- Seat additions within Professional are billed immediately on a prorated basis and reflected in the next invoice

### Downgrade Rules
- Downgrades from Professional to Starter are permitted at the next renewal date only
- Accounts must bring usage (seats, storage, active integrations) within Starter plan limits prior to downgrade taking effect; Corvex Customer Success will flag any features that will be lost
- Data exceeding Starter's storage or retention limits is archived, not deleted, for 90 days following downgrade

### Billing Frequency
- Monthly or annual, selected at signup or at any renewal
- Switching from monthly to annual billing may be done at any time and takes effect at the next billing date
- Switching from annual to monthly billing takes effect at the end of the current annual term

### Cancellation Terms
- Cancel anytime from account settings or by contacting Customer Success; no cancellation fee
- Monthly plans: cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid month
- Annual plans: cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid annual term; no partial-year refunds are issued except as required by applicable law
- Accounts under an active annual contract may request early termination, subject to the terms of their signed order form

## 5. Enterprise Plan

### Monthly Price
**Custom pricing**, quoted based on seat count, feature configuration, and contract term. Enterprise is not available on standard month-to-month billing; see Billing Frequency below.

### Annual Price
**Custom pricing**, negotiated per account based on seat volume, usage tier, add-ons (such as expanded automation capacity), and contract length. Multi-year agreements are available at a further discount. Prospective Enterprise customers receive a formal quote from the Corvex sales team following a needs assessment.

### Included Features
- Everything in Professional, plus:
- Advanced AI-assisted automation and workflow capabilities at expanded usage capacity
- Custom SLAs for platform uptime and support response
- Dedicated infrastructure options for customers with strict isolation requirements
- Sandbox/staging environment for testing configuration changes
- Custom reporting and data warehouse export
- Unlimited help center languages and multi-brand help center support

### User Limits
- Minimum 25 seats
- No maximum; large deployments (500+ seats) are supported with dedicated onboarding

### Storage Limits
- Custom storage allocation, scoped to account needs during contracting
- Custom case history retention (including extended retention for regulated industries)

### API Access
- Full read/write API access with elevated default rate limits (custom limits available on request)
- Priority API support and access to early-access API endpoints
- Dedicated webhook infrastructure available for high-volume accounts

### Support Level
- 24/7 priority support with a target first response within 1 hour for urgent issues
- Dedicated, named Customer Success Manager and Technical Account Manager
- Custom onboarding, implementation support, and quarterly business reviews

### Integrations
- All Professional integrations, plus custom integration development support
- Access to the Corvex partner API for bespoke internal system connections
- Priority consideration for new integration requests

### Security Features
- Everything in Professional, plus:
- Advanced audit logging with extended (custom) retention
- Data residency options for regional compliance requirements
- Support for additional SSO protocols and SCIM-based user provisioning
- Custom data processing agreements and compliance documentation available on request

### Upgrade Rules
- Enterprise is the top tier; "upgrades" consist of expanding seats, storage, or add-on capacity within the existing agreement
- Mid-contract expansions are handled through an order form addendum and billed on a prorated basis aligned to the existing contract term

### Downgrade Rules
- Downgrades from Enterprise to Professional are evaluated on a case-by-case basis with the assigned Customer Success Manager, given the custom nature of Enterprise configurations
- Any downgrade takes effect only at contract renewal, not mid-term
- Data and configuration exceeding the destination plan's limits will be reviewed with the customer prior to downgrade to determine an appropriate migration or archival path

### Billing Frequency
- Enterprise accounts are billed annually or per the schedule defined in their signed order form (including multi-year terms where applicable)
- Invoicing terms (e.g., net-30) are negotiated as part of the contracting process

### Cancellation Terms
- Enterprise agreements are governed by the terms of the signed order form and master subscription agreement
- Standard Enterprise contracts do not auto-renew silently; customers receive advance notice ahead of renewal per their contract terms
- Early termination terms, if any, are defined in the individual customer agreement

## 6. Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

**Does Corvex charge based on ticket or conversation volume?**
No. Corvex's core pricing is seat-based, not volume-based. Certain automation and AI-assisted features may draw from a usage-based allowance on Professional and Enterprise plans, but base case/ticket handling is not metered.

**What counts as a "seat"?**
A seat is any named individual with login access to the Corvex agent workspace — this includes support agents, team leads, and administrators. Customers or end users contacting support are never counted as seats.

**Can I mix billing frequencies across different parts of my account?**
No. A single Corvex account is billed on one frequency (monthly or annual) at a time. Enterprise contracts may include custom schedules as defined in the order form.

**What happens if I exceed my plan's storage limit?**
Starter and Professional accounts approaching their storage limit receive an in-app and email notification. Continued growth beyond the limit may require an add-on purchase (Professional) or an upgrade (Starter); accounts are never abruptly cut off without notice.

**Is there a free trial?**
Starter and Professional plans are available with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required to begin. Enterprise plans do not use a self-serve trial; prospective Enterprise customers typically evaluate the platform through a guided proof-of-concept managed with the sales team.

**Do you offer discounts for nonprofits, startups, or educational institutions?**
Corvex offers a standard nonprofit discount on Professional plans, applied after verification of nonprofit status. Startup and educational discounts are evaluated on a case-by-case basis by the sales team.

**What happens to my data if I cancel?**
Following cancellation, account data is retained in an inactive, exportable state for 30 days, after which it is permanently deleted in accordance with Corvex's data retention practices. Customers are encouraged to export any needed data prior to cancellation.

**Can I switch from Starter directly to Enterprise?**
Yes. Plan changes are not required to move sequentially through tiers — an account may upgrade directly from Starter to Enterprise if it better fits the organization's needs.

**Are add-ons available across all plans?**
Most usage-based add-ons (such as expanded automation capacity or additional storage) are available on Professional and Enterprise plans. Starter is intentionally limited to its included allowances to keep the entry tier simple and predictably priced.

**Will my price change if Corvex updates its pricing in the future?**
Existing customers on an active contract term (monthly or annual) are not affected by list price changes until their next renewal. Corvex provides advance notice of any pricing changes ahead of a customer's renewal date.

**Who do I contact with billing questions?**
Starter and Professional customers can reach billing support through the in-app help widget or by emailing billing support directly. Enterprise customers should contact their assigned Customer Success Manager or Technical Account Manager.
