## 1. Introduction

Welcome to CloudDesk Analytics, the reporting and business intelligence module of the Corvex Cloud platform. This manual is designed to help new and existing users — team leads, administrators, and reporting users — become comfortable and productive with CloudDesk Analytics as quickly as possible.

CloudDesk Analytics draws directly from the same unified case data layer used by CloudDesk Chat and CloudDesk Tickets, turning every chat and ticket your team handles into structured, real-time reporting. Whether you need a live view of today's queue or a defensible quarter-over-quarter performance report, CloudDesk Analytics is built to serve both from the same underlying, always-current data.

This manual assumes you already have an active Corvex Cloud account with CloudDesk Chat and/or CloudDesk Tickets in use, since CloudDesk Analytics reports on data generated by those modules. If your organization has not yet set up a Corvex Cloud account, contact your Corvex account representative or visit the Corvex Cloud website to begin a trial.

Throughout this manual, instructions apply to the standard web-based dashboard experience unless otherwise noted. Mobile-specific instructions are covered separately in the CloudDesk Mobile documentation.

## 2. Installation

CloudDesk Analytics is a cloud-hosted application. There is no software to download or install — you access it entirely through your web browser. Because CloudDesk Analytics reports on data generated elsewhere in Corvex Cloud, there is no separate "installation" step for the reporting module itself; setup instead consists of confirming your data sources and, where applicable, connecting export destinations.

### 2.1 Confirming Data Sources

CloudDesk Analytics automatically reports on any active CloudDesk Chat and CloudDesk Tickets data on your account. No manual configuration is required for this baseline reporting to begin appearing.

### 2.2 Connecting Scheduled Report Delivery (Optional)

1. Log in to CloudDesk Analytics as an administrator.
2. Navigate to **Settings > Reports > Scheduled Delivery**.
3. Add recipient email addresses and configure a delivery schedule for any standard or custom report you'd like distributed automatically. Available on Professional and Enterprise plans.

### 2.3 Connecting Data Warehouse Export (Enterprise Only)

1. Navigate to **Settings > Reports > Data Warehouse Export**.
2. Follow the guided setup to authenticate your destination business intelligence environment.
3. Select which datasets to include in the export and confirm the export schedule.

### 2.4 Reporting User Access

No installation is required for team leads, administrators, or reporting users to use CloudDesk Analytics. Once your account has been created or granted reporting access by an administrator, you can log in from any supported web browser, as described in Section 4.

## 3. System Requirements

### 3.1 For Team Leads, Administrators, and Reporting Users

- A supported desktop web browser: current or prior major version of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, or Microsoft Edge
- A stable internet connection
- A minimum screen resolution of 1280×800 is recommended for the best dashboard layout experience, though the workspace is responsive down to smaller screens
- No local software installation, plugins, or browser extensions are required

- CloudDesk Mobile app on a currently supported iOS or Android release, for condensed dashboard snapshots (full detail covered in the CloudDesk Mobile User Manual)

### 3.3 For Data Warehouse Export (Enterprise Only)

- A supported business intelligence or data warehouse environment capable of receiving structured data exports, as detailed in the Corvex Cloud developer documentation

## 4. First Login

### 4.1 Receiving Access

Team leads, administrators, and reporting users are granted access to CloudDesk Analytics by an existing administrator on your account. If you already use CloudDesk Chat or CloudDesk Tickets, CloudDesk Analytics is typically accessible from the same account without a separate invitation; if you are a reporting-only user, you will receive a standard email invitation.

### 4.2 Setting Up Your Account (Reporting-Only Users)

1. Open the invitation email and click **Accept Invitation**.
2. You will be directed to the Corvex Cloud account setup page.
3. Create a password meeting your organization's password policy, or, if your organization uses Single Sign-On (SAML 2.0), you will instead be directed to authenticate through your organization's identity provider.
4. Confirm your name and time zone.
5. Click **Complete Setup**.

### 4.3 Logging In

1. Navigate to your organization's CloudDesk Analytics login page (typically `[yourcompany].corvexcloud.com`, or your organization's custom domain, if configured).
2. Enter your email address and password, or select **Sign in with SSO** if your organization uses Single Sign-On.
3. Click **Log In**.

Upon your first successful login, you will be presented with a brief guided walkthrough of the dashboard experience. You can skip this walkthrough at any time and revisit it later from **Help > Getting Started**.

## 5. Dashboard Overview

Once logged in, you land on the CloudDesk Analytics home dashboard. The dashboard is organized into four main areas:

### 5.1 Left Navigation Panel

A persistent vertical panel on the left side of the screen, providing access to the Real-Time view, Standard Reports, Custom Dashboards, and Settings.

### 5.2 Metric Summary Bar

A row of key metric summaries at the top of the dashboard — such as open ticket count, active chats, and average response time — giving an at-a-glance status check before diving into detail.

### 5.3 Main Report Area

The center of the screen displays the currently selected view: the real-time queue dashboard, a standard report, or a custom dashboard, depending on your navigation selection.

### 5.4 Filter and Date Range Panel

A panel above the Main Report Area allowing you to adjust the date range, channel, team, or tag filters applied to the current view. Available filter options vary by plan tier, as described in the CloudDesk Analytics Product Overview.

---

## 6. Navigation

### 6.1 Moving Between Sections

Use the left navigation panel to move between the main sections of CloudDesk Analytics:

- **Real-Time** — the live queue dashboard, reflecting current ticket and chat status
- **Standard Reports** — pre-built reports covering volume, response time, resolution time, and CSAT
- **Custom Dashboards** — (Professional and Enterprise) dashboards you or a colleague have built and saved
- **Settings** — report access permissions, scheduled delivery, and export configuration (Administrator role)

### 6.2 Switching Between Reports and Dashboards

Click any report or dashboard name from the relevant section to open it in the Main Report Area. Recently viewed reports appear at the top of each section for quick access.

### 6.3 Adjusting Date Ranges

Use the Filter and Date Range Panel to select a preset range (such as Today, Last 7 Days, or Last 30 Days) or, on Professional and Enterprise plans, a fully custom date range.

### 6.4 Saving Views

When you've configured a set of filters you expect to reuse, use **Save View** (Professional and Enterprise) to store it for quick access later, without needing to reapply the same filters each time.

## 7. User Settings

Access your personal settings by clicking your profile icon in the bottom-left corner of the screen and selecting **My Settings**.

### 7.1 Profile

Update your display name, profile photo, and time zone.

### 7.2 Notification Preferences

Configure alerts related to reporting:

- **Scheduled report delivery** — choose which scheduled reports, if any, are sent to your email
- **Threshold alerts** — (Professional and Enterprise) receive a notification when a defined metric crosses a set threshold

### 7.3 Default Landing View

Set whether CloudDesk Analytics opens to the Real-Time view, a specific Standard Report, or a saved Custom Dashboard when you log in.

### 7.4 Saved Views and Dashboards

Manage the views and dashboards you've personally created or saved from **My Settings > Saved Views**.

### 7.5 Password and Security

Change your password (if not using SSO) from this section.

## 8. Creating Your First Dashboard

If you are a team lead or administrator building your first custom dashboard, this section walks through the process. Custom dashboards are available on Professional and Enterprise plans; Starter plan users can follow along using the standard report configuration options available to them.

### 8.1 Step 1: Start a New Dashboard

1. Navigate to **Custom Dashboards**.
2. Click **Create Dashboard**.
3. Give your dashboard a clear, descriptive name (for example, "Weekly Team Performance" or "E-Commerce Support Trends").

### 8.2 Step 2: Add Widgets

1. Click **Add Widget** and choose a metric type: volume, response time, resolution time, CSAT/NPS, or a custom combination.
2. Select the channel(s) to include — chat, tickets, or both.
3. Choose a visualization style (line chart, bar chart, or summary number) appropriate to the metric.
4. Repeat to add additional widgets to your dashboard.

### 8.3 Step 3: Apply Filters

1. Apply filters relevant to your dashboard's purpose — for example, filtering to a specific team, tag, or priority level.
2. Set a default date range for the dashboard; viewers can adjust this later unless you lock the range.

### 8.4 Step 4: Arrange and Save

1. Drag and drop widgets to arrange the dashboard layout.
2. Click **Save Dashboard**.
3. Choose whether the dashboard is private to you or shared with your team or specific roles.

### 8.5 Step 5: Schedule Delivery (Optional)

1. From the saved dashboard, click **Schedule Delivery**.
2. Choose recipients and a delivery frequency (daily, weekly, or monthly).
3. Confirm — recipients will now receive this dashboard's summary automatically, without needing to log in.

## 9. Daily Workflow

A typical day using CloudDesk Analytics as a team lead generally follows this pattern:

### 9.1 Morning Check-In

1. Log in and review the **Real-Time** dashboard for current queue status, wait times, and agent availability.
2. Check for any threshold alerts received overnight or since your last login.
3. Review yesterday's Standard Reports summary if your team tracks day-over-day performance.

### 9.2 Throughout the Day

1. Periodically revisit the Real-Time dashboard to catch workload imbalances or SLA risk before it affects customers.
2. Use saved views or custom dashboards relevant to your specific area of oversight (for example, a specific channel or team).
3. Respond to threshold alerts by reallocating workload or escalating to an administrator, as appropriate.

### 9.3 End-of-Day or End-of-Week Review

1. Review Standard Reports or custom dashboards covering the full day or week, depending on your team's cadence.
2. Export or share relevant reports with stakeholders who need visibility but don't log in to CloudDesk Analytics directly.
3. Note any recurring trends worth flagging to CX leadership or Product/Engineering.

### 9.4 Periodic Deep-Dive Analysis

On a weekly or monthly basis, many team leads and CX leaders use custom date ranges to review longer-term trends — staffing needs, recurring issue categories, or CSAT trajectory — feeding into staffing plans and process improvements.

## 10. Best Practices

- **Check the Real-Time dashboard before assuming a report tells the whole story.** Standard and custom reports are excellent for trends, but same-day operational decisions should be grounded in real-time data.
- **Standardize your team's tagging before building custom dashboards.** A dashboard is only as useful as the consistency of the data feeding it — inconsistent tagging in CloudDesk Chat or CloudDesk Tickets will show up as noise here.
- **Use saved views instead of rebuilding filters.** If you find yourself applying the same filters repeatedly, save that view rather than reconstructing it each time.
- **Share dashboards, don't just screenshot them.** A shared, live dashboard stays accurate as data updates; a screenshot is outdated the moment it's sent.
- **Set threshold alerts thoughtfully.** Alerts that fire too often lose their value; calibrate thresholds to genuinely actionable conditions.
- **Revisit custom dashboards periodically.** As team structure or priorities change, dashboards built for a previous configuration can quietly become less useful if not maintained.

---

## 11. Tips

- Use the Metric Summary Bar as your first stop each login — it's designed to surface the handful of numbers that matter most before you dig into any specific report.
- When building a dashboard for executive stakeholders, favor summary numbers and simple trend lines over dense, highly detailed widgets.
- Combine channel filters (chat and tickets together) when you want a true picture of total support workload, rather than viewing each channel in isolation.
- Use scheduled delivery for any dashboard a stakeholder needs regularly but doesn't need to interact with directly — it reduces both their effort and yours.
- If you manage multiple teams or regions (Enterprise), use cross-team or cross-region reporting to compare performance rather than toggling between separate dashboards.

## 14. Logging Out

To log out of CloudDesk Analytics:

1. Click your profile icon in the bottom-left corner of the screen.
2. Select **Log Out** from the menu.
3. You will be returned to the CloudDesk Analytics login screen.

### 14.1 Before You Log Out

As a best practice, before logging out:

- Ensure any dashboard changes you made are saved
- Confirm scheduled report deliveries you configured are active and correctly addressed
- Note any trends or alerts you plan to follow up on so they aren't lost between sessions

### 14.2 Automatic Logout

For security purposes, your session may automatically log out after a period of inactivity, as configured by your administrator. If Single Sign-On is enabled for your organization, your session behavior may also be governed by your identity provider's session policies.
