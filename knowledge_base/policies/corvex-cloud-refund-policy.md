# Corvex Technologies, Inc.
## Refund Policy

**Document Type:** Enterprise Policy
**Applies To:** Corvex Cloud (CloudDesk Chat, CloudDesk Tickets, CloudDesk Analytics, CloudDesk API Platform, CloudDesk Mobile)
**Effective Date:** Current as of publication
**Owner:** Finance & Operations, in coordination with Legal & Compliance

---

## 1. Purpose

This Refund Policy establishes the official rules governing refunds, credits, and related billing adjustments for subscriptions to Corvex Cloud. Its purpose is to:

- Provide customers, prospective customers, and internal Corvex teams with a single, authoritative reference for how refund requests are evaluated and processed
- Ensure refund decisions are applied consistently across Starter, Professional, and Enterprise accounts
- Clarify the relationship between this policy and the cancellation, billing, and plan-change terms described in the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide
- Reduce ambiguity for Corvex's Finance & Operations, Customer Success, and Support teams when responding to billing inquiries

This policy governs monetary refunds and credits specifically. It does not govern general cancellation mechanics (how and when a subscription ends), which are addressed in the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide, or data retention following account closure, which is addressed separately in that same document.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All paid subscriptions to Corvex Cloud, across the Starter, Professional, and Enterprise plans
- All billing frequencies, including monthly, annual, and multi-year Enterprise terms
- Usage-based add-on charges billed in connection with a Corvex Cloud subscription
- All customers, regardless of geographic region, subject to Section 6 (Compliance) regarding jurisdiction-specific legal requirements that may supersede specific provisions of this policy

This policy does not apply to:

- Free trial usage, which involves no payment and therefore no refund consideration
- Third-party charges not billed directly by Corvex Technologies, Inc. (for example, a separately contracted systems integrator's implementation fees)
- Non-monetary account matters such as data export, data deletion, or account reactivation, which are addressed in the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide and the relevant Administrator Guide

## 3. Definitions

**Billing Period:** The span of time for which a customer has been charged in advance — one calendar month for monthly billing, one year for standard annual billing, or the term defined in a signed Enterprise order form for multi-year agreements.

**Credit:** A non-cash adjustment applied to a customer's account, reducing the amount owed on a future invoice, as distinguished from a Refund, which returns funds to the customer's original payment method.

**Order Form:** The signed contractual document governing an Enterprise customer's specific pricing, term length, and billing arrangement, which takes precedence over standard plan terms where the two conflict.

**Refund:** A monetary return of previously collected payment to the customer's original payment method.

**Refund Request:** A customer's formal submission, through a Corvex-designated channel, asking for a Refund or Credit related to a specific charge.

**Seat:** A named individual license to the Corvex Cloud platform, as defined in the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide, used as the basis for most subscription billing.

**Service Disruption:** A period during which Corvex Cloud is unavailable or materially degraded due to a failure attributable to Corvex's infrastructure, as distinct from issues caused by a customer's own configuration, integration, or network environment.

**Standard Plan:** Refers collectively to the Starter and Professional plans, billed under standard, published pricing terms, as distinguished from Enterprise, which is governed by individually negotiated order forms.

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 General Refund Position

Corvex Technologies' general position is that subscription fees are earned upon the start of each Billing Period, consistent with the terms disclosed at signup and in the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide. As a result, Corvex does not, as a general rule, issue prorated or partial refunds for:

- Voluntary cancellation partway through a paid Billing Period
- A downgrade to a lower plan tier partway through a paid Billing Period
- A reduction in seat count partway through a paid Billing Period
- Underutilization of a subscription (for example, seats purchased but not actively used by the customer's team)

This general position applies uniformly across Starter, Professional, and Enterprise plans, and is consistent with the cancellation terms described in the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide.

### 4.2 Monthly Subscriptions

For monthly billed Standard Plan subscriptions:

- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid month
- No refund is issued for the remainder of a partially used month following cancellation
- No cancellation fee applies

### 4.3 Annual Subscriptions

For annual billed Standard Plan subscriptions:

- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid annual term
- No partial-year refund is issued for the unused portion of an annual term following cancellation, except as required by applicable law (see Section 6)
- No cancellation fee applies

### 4.4 Enterprise Subscriptions

Enterprise refund terms are governed primarily by the customer's signed Order Form, which takes precedence over this policy where the two conflict. In the absence of specific Order Form terms addressing a given situation, the general provisions of this policy apply. Enterprise customers with questions about their specific contractual refund terms should contact their Customer Success Manager or the Corvex billing team.

### 4.5 Billing Errors

Corvex will issue a full refund or credit, at the customer's preference, for any charge resulting from a verified Corvex billing error, including but not limited to:

- Duplicate charges for the same Billing Period
- Charges continuing after a properly submitted and confirmed cancellation
- Incorrect seat count billing not reflecting the customer's actual, confirmed seat configuration at the time of billing
- Charges applied at an incorrect rate inconsistent with the customer's confirmed plan and billing frequency

Billing error refunds are not subject to the general non-refund position described in Section 4.1, since they represent a correction of an error rather than a discretionary accommodation.

### 4.6 Service Disruption Credits

In the event of a significant, sustained Service Disruption attributable to Corvex's infrastructure, affected customers may be eligible for a service credit applied to a future invoice, in accordance with the uptime and reliability commitments described in the customer's plan tier or, for Enterprise customers, their specific Order Form's service level terms. Service Disruption credits are issued as Credits, not cash Refunds, except where an Enterprise Order Form specifies otherwise.

Service Disruption credit eligibility does not extend to:

- Degraded performance caused by a customer's own website, network, integration configuration, or third-party service
- Scheduled maintenance communicated in advance through the Corvex Cloud status page
- Issues affecting a single customer's account configuration rather than the broader Corvex Cloud infrastructure

### 4.7 Downgrade and Plan Change Refunds

Consistent with the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide, plan upgrades are billed immediately on a prorated basis and are not eligible for refund once the upgraded features and pricing take effect. Downgrades take effect at the customer's next renewal date rather than immediately, and accordingly do not generate a mid-cycle refund, since the customer continues to receive service at the higher tier through the remainder of the then-current Billing Period.

### 4.8 Trial-to-Paid Conversion

Because Corvex's standard trial period involves no payment, no refund is applicable to the trial period itself. If a customer is inadvertently charged before a trial period has concluded due to a confirmed billing error, Section 4.5 applies.

### 4.9 Refunds for Add-On Usage Charges

Usage-based add-on charges (for example, expanded automation capacity) are billed based on actual, metered usage during the relevant Billing Period and are not eligible for refund on the basis of a customer later deciding the usage was unnecessary. A billing error affecting metered usage charges is addressed under Section 4.5.

### 4.10 Method of Refund

Approved Refunds are returned to the original payment method used for the applicable charge wherever technically possible. If the original payment method is no longer valid or available (for example, an expired card), Corvex will coordinate an alternative refund method with the customer's designated billing contact.

### 4.11 Timing of Refunds

Approved Refunds are typically processed within 10 business days of approval, though the time for funds to appear in the customer's account may vary depending on the customer's financial institution or payment provider.

## 5. Responsibilities

### 5.1 Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- Submitting Refund Requests through an official Corvex support channel, with sufficient detail to identify the specific charge in question
- Maintaining accurate, current billing contact information under Admin Dashboard > Billing & Plan
- Reviewing seat usage, plan tier, and billing frequency regularly to avoid unintended charges, as encouraged in the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide
- For Enterprise customers, referring to their specific Order Form for any refund terms that differ from this general policy

### 5.2 Corvex Finance & Operations Responsibilities

The Finance & Operations department is responsible for:

- Reviewing and processing Refund Requests according to this policy
- Verifying billing errors reported under Section 4.5 against internal billing system records
- Processing approved Refunds within the timeframe described in Section 4.11
- Maintaining records of all Refund Requests and their outcomes for internal audit and compliance purposes

### 5.3 Customer Success Responsibilities

Customer Success Managers, particularly for Enterprise accounts, are responsible for:

- Serving as the first point of contact for Enterprise billing and refund inquiries
- Coordinating with Finance & Operations on Order Form-specific refund terms
- Communicating Service Disruption credit eligibility to affected customers where applicable

### 5.4 Legal & Compliance Responsibilities

The Legal & Compliance department is responsible for:

- Reviewing this policy periodically for consistency with applicable consumer protection and billing regulations across the jurisdictions in which Corvex operates
- Advising Finance & Operations on jurisdiction-specific exceptions required under Section 6
- Approving any Order Form language that varies from this standard policy for Enterprise customers

## 6. Exceptions

### 6.1 Legally Mandated Refunds

Where applicable law in a customer's jurisdiction requires a refund, cooling-off period, or other consumer protection provision that conflicts with this policy, the legally mandated requirement takes precedence, and Corvex will comply accordingly. Customers who believe such a legal requirement applies to their situation should note this specifically in their Refund Request.

### 6.2 Discretionary Exceptions

Corvex Finance & Operations, in consultation with Customer Success for the relevant account, may approve a discretionary exception to the general provisions of Section 4.1 in circumstances such as:

- A documented, exceptional hardship affecting the customer's business
- A significant, demonstrated gap between the service the customer reasonably expected based on Corvex's own representations and the service actually delivered, not otherwise addressed under Section 4.5 or 4.6
- Other circumstances Corvex determines, at its sole discretion, warrant an exception in the interest of a fair customer outcome

Discretionary exceptions are evaluated on a case-by-case basis and do not establish precedent for future requests, whether from the same customer or others.

### 6.3 Enterprise Order Form Precedence

As described in Section 4.4, an Enterprise customer's signed Order Form takes precedence over this policy for any term the Order Form specifically addresses. This policy fills gaps not addressed by the Order Form rather than overriding it.

### 6.4 Fraud and Abuse

Corvex reserves the right to deny a Refund Request, or to reverse a previously issued Refund, where there is a reasonable basis to conclude the request involves fraud, abuse of this policy, or a violation of Corvex's other usage terms.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I get a refund if I cancel my subscription early?**
Generally, no. Monthly subscriptions run through the end of the current paid month, and annual subscriptions run through the end of the current paid annual term, with no partial refund for the unused portion, except where required by law or where a billing error applies.

**What if I was charged after I cancelled?**
This is treated as a billing error under Section 4.5 and is eligible for a full refund once verified. Contact Corvex billing support with your cancellation confirmation details.

**I upgraded my plan but want to go back — can I get a refund for the difference?**
Upgrades are billed immediately and are not refundable once the upgraded plan and its features take effect. You may downgrade going forward, which takes effect at your next renewal, consistent with the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide.

**Do you offer refunds for seats we purchased but didn't end up using?**
No. Refunds are not issued on the basis of underutilization of purchased seats. We recommend reviewing your seat usage regularly, as described in the CloudDesk Chat Administrator Guide, to right-size your account before renewal.

**What happens if Corvex Cloud goes down for an extended period?**
Depending on the nature and duration of the disruption and your plan or Order Form's service level terms, you may be eligible for a service credit applied to a future invoice, as described in Section 4.6.

**How long does it take to receive a refund once approved?**
Approved refunds are typically processed within 10 business days, though your financial institution may take additional time to reflect the funds in your account.

**Will a refund be issued to my original payment method?**
Yes, wherever technically possible. If your original payment method is no longer valid, Corvex will work with your billing contact to arrange an alternative method.

**Does this policy apply to Enterprise customers?**
Enterprise refund terms are primarily governed by your signed Order Form. This policy applies to any situation your Order Form does not specifically address.

**Who do I contact to request a refund?**
Starter and Professional customers should contact Corvex billing support through the in-app help widget or billing support email. Enterprise customers should contact their assigned Customer Success Manager.

**Can Corvex deny a refund request?**
Yes. Requests may be denied where they fall outside the provisions of this policy, or where Corvex determines the request involves fraud or abuse, as described in Section 6.4.
