# Corvex Technologies, Inc.
## Privacy Policy

**Document Type:** Enterprise Policy
**Applies To:** Corvex Cloud (CloudDesk Chat, CloudDesk Tickets, CloudDesk Analytics, CloudDesk API Platform, CloudDesk Mobile) and the corvexcloud.com website
**Effective Date:** Current as of publication
**Owner:** Legal & Compliance, in coordination with Security & Trust

---

## 1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how Corvex Technologies, Inc. ("Corvex," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data in connection with the Corvex Cloud platform and related services. Its purpose is to:

- Provide customers, their end users, prospective customers, and website visitors with a clear, accurate account of Corvex's data handling practices
- Establish internal accountability for how personal data is collected, processed, stored, and shared across Corvex's products and business functions
- Support Corvex's commitment to transparency, as reflected in the "default to transparency" value described in the Corvex Technologies Company Overview
- Serve as the governing reference for privacy-related decisions made by Corvex's Security & Trust and Legal & Compliance teams

This policy addresses personal data handling broadly. It does not address monetary billing matters, which are addressed in the Refund Policy, or general subscription and cancellation terms, which are addressed in the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide.

---

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to personal data Corvex processes in connection with:

- **Customer accounts:** Individuals at customer organizations who use Corvex Cloud as agents, team leads, administrators, or other authorized users ("Customer Users")
- **End users:** Individuals who interact with a Corvex customer's support operations through CloudDesk Chat, CloudDesk Tickets, or related channels ("End Users") — for example, a customer's own shoppers, account holders, or support requesters
- **Website visitors:** Individuals who visit corvexcloud.com, including prospective customers
- **Corvex personnel:** Employees and contractors, to the extent this policy addresses data handling practices relevant to how Corvex operates its business (separate internal HR policies govern employee data in greater depth)

### 2.2 Products Covered

This policy applies across all Corvex Cloud modules: CloudDesk Chat, CloudDesk Tickets, CloudDesk Analytics, CloudDesk API Platform, and CloudDesk Mobile, all of which operate on the same underlying account and data infrastructure.

### 2.3 Relationship to Customer Obligations

For data about End Users submitted to Corvex Cloud by a customer (for example, a customer's shopper contacting support through CloudDesk Chat), the customer is generally the data controller, and Corvex acts as a data processor providing the underlying platform. Customers are responsible for ensuring they have an appropriate legal basis to collect and share such End User data with Corvex, and for providing their own End Users with appropriate privacy disclosures. This policy describes how Corvex, as processor, handles that data on the customer's behalf, in addition to describing Corvex's practices as a controller of Customer User and website visitor data.

---

## 3. Definitions

**Controller:** The entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data. For End User data submitted through a customer's use of Corvex Cloud, the customer is generally the Controller.

**Customer User:** An individual at a customer organization authorized to access Corvex Cloud as an agent, team lead, administrator, or similar role.

**End User:** An individual who interacts with a Corvex customer's support operations (for example, through a chat widget, ticket submission, or web form) but who is not themselves a Corvex Cloud account holder.

**Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, including but not limited to name, email address, IP address, and any other information submitted through Corvex Cloud that could reasonably identify a specific person.

**Processor:** An entity that processes personal data on behalf of, and under the instructions of, a Controller. Corvex acts as a Processor with respect to End User data submitted by customers.

**Processing:** Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, and deletion.

**Sub-processor:** A third-party service provider engaged by Corvex to assist in processing personal data on Corvex's behalf, operating under contractual obligations consistent with this policy.

**Website Visitor:** An individual who visits corvexcloud.com without necessarily becoming a customer or Customer User.

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Categories of Personal Data Collected

Depending on your relationship to Corvex, we may collect:

- **Account and contact information:** Name, email address, job title, and organization name, collected when a Customer User is invited to or creates a Corvex Cloud account
- **Authentication data:** Login credentials or, where Single Sign-On is used, authentication tokens exchanged with your organization's identity provider
- **End User data submitted through the platform:** Names, email addresses, and message content submitted by End Users through CloudDesk Chat, CloudDesk Tickets, or connected channels, on behalf of and at the direction of the customer
- **Usage data:** Information about how the platform is used, such as feature interaction, login times, and general activity patterns, used to operate, maintain, and improve the service
- **Device and technical data:** IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system, collected automatically when using Corvex Cloud or corvexcloud.com
- **Website visitor data:** Information submitted through forms on corvexcloud.com, such as a request for a product demonstration, and standard web analytics data

### 4.2 How Personal Data Is Used

Corvex uses personal data to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the Corvex Cloud platform, including case management, reporting, and automation functionality
- Authenticate users and secure account access
- Communicate with Customer Users about their account, including service notices, security alerts, and, where consented to, product updates
- Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
- Improve and develop the platform, including through aggregated or de-identified usage analysis
- Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
- Comply with legal obligations

Corvex does not use End User data submitted through a customer's account for Corvex's own independent marketing purposes; such data is processed solely to provide the platform service to the customer, consistent with our role as Processor.

### 4.3 Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable law requires a specific legal basis for processing, Corvex relies on one or more of the following, depending on the specific processing activity: performance of a contract (providing the service a customer has subscribed to), legitimate interests (such as securing the platform or improving the service), compliance with a legal obligation, or, where applicable, consent (such as for optional marketing communications).

### 4.4 How Personal Data Is Shared

Corvex shares personal data only in the following circumstances:

- **With Sub-processors:** Corvex engages Sub-processors to provide underlying infrastructure, hosting, and specific supporting functions (for example, email delivery for platform notifications). Sub-processors are contractually bound to data protection obligations consistent with this policy and are used solely to provide services to Corvex, not for their own independent purposes.
- **With integrations a customer configures:** When a customer connects a CRM, e-commerce platform, or other integration under Admin Dashboard > Integrations, relevant data is shared with that connected system according to the customer's own configuration and instruction, not at Corvex's independent discretion.
- **For legal reasons:** Where required to comply with applicable law, respond to a valid legal process, or protect the rights, property, or safety of Corvex, its customers, or others.
- **In connection with a business transaction:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to continued protection consistent with this policy or as required by applicable law.

Consistent with the Corvex Technologies Company Overview, Corvex does not sell personal data to third parties, and does not use customer or End User data for third-party advertising purposes.

### 4.5 Data Security

Corvex applies security measures described in the CloudDesk Chat Administrator Guide and consistent across the platform, including encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256), role-based access controls, and audit logging of security-relevant activity. Additional security capabilities, including Single Sign-On, IP allowlisting, and SCIM-based provisioning, are available to customers as described in the relevant Administrator Guide and the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide.

### 4.6 Data Retention

Personal data submitted through Corvex Cloud is retained according to the case history retention periods defined by the customer's plan tier, as described in the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide: 18 months on Starter, 36 months on Professional, and custom retention (including extended retention for regulated industries) on Enterprise. Following account cancellation, data is retained in an inactive, exportable state for 30 days before permanent deletion, as described in the Refund Policy and the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide.

Corvex Customer User account data (such as login credentials) is retained for the duration of the account's active status and is removed following account deactivation and any applicable internal retention period required for security or legal recordkeeping purposes.

### 4.7 Data Residency

Enterprise customers with regional compliance requirements may request specific data residency configurations, established during contracting with their Customer Success Manager, as described in the CloudDesk Chat Administrator Guide. Absent such an arrangement, customer data is hosted within Corvex's standard, primary infrastructure regions.

### 4.8 International Data Transfers

Because Corvex operates globally, including hub offices in Austin, Dublin, and Singapore, personal data may be processed in a country other than the one in which it was originally collected. Where such transfers occur, Corvex uses appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable data protection law governing international transfers.

### 4.9 Data Subject Rights

Depending on applicable law in your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, or to receive a copy of it in a portable format. Requests related to End User data should generally be directed to the relevant Corvex customer, since the customer is typically the Controller of that data; Corvex will support customers in fulfilling such requests as their Processor. Requests related to Customer User account data or website visitor data may be submitted directly to Corvex through the contact information in Section 10.

### 4.10 Cookies and Similar Technologies

corvexcloud.com and the Corvex Cloud platform use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, security, and understanding how the website and platform are used. Where required by applicable law, Corvex provides a mechanism for website visitors to manage non-essential cookie preferences.

### 4.11 Children's Data

Corvex Cloud is designed for use by business customers and their support operations and is not directed at children. Corvex does not knowingly collect personal data from children through corvexcloud.com or the Corvex Cloud platform in a manner inconsistent with applicable law.

## 5. Responsibilities

### 5.1 Corvex Legal & Compliance

Legal & Compliance is responsible for:

- Maintaining the accuracy and currency of this policy
- Reviewing Sub-processor agreements for consistency with this policy's data protection commitments
- Responding to data subject rights requests directed to Corvex under Section 4.9
- Advising other departments on applicable privacy law requirements

### 5.2 Corvex Security & Trust

Security & Trust is responsible for:

- Implementing and maintaining the technical security measures described in Section 4.5
- Investigating and responding to any suspected data security incident
- Reviewing Sub-processor security practices as part of vendor risk management

### 5.3 Corvex Product and Engineering

Product and Engineering teams are responsible for:

- Building platform features consistent with the data handling commitments described in this policy
- Ensuring new features undergo privacy-relevant review before release, particularly where they introduce new categories of data collection

### 5.4 Customers (as Controllers of End User Data)

Customers using Corvex Cloud are responsible for:

- Ensuring they have an appropriate legal basis to collect and submit End User data through Corvex Cloud
- Providing their own End Users with appropriate privacy disclosures regarding the customer's own use of Corvex Cloud
- Configuring their account's data handling settings (such as retention and integration configuration) consistent with their own legal obligations
- Responding to, or coordinating with Corvex to fulfill, End User data subject rights requests

### 5.5 Customer Users

Individual Customer Users are responsible for:

- Maintaining the confidentiality of their own login credentials
- Using the platform consistent with their organization's own policies and applicable law
- Reporting any suspected unauthorized access to their account to their organization's administrator

## 6. Exceptions

### 6.1 Legal Process

Corvex may disclose personal data beyond the scope otherwise described in this policy where required to comply with a valid subpoena, court order, or other legal process, or where necessary to protect the safety of any person, subject to Corvex's standard practice of notifying the affected customer where legally permitted to do so.

### 6.2 Aggregated and De-Identified Data

This policy's provisions regarding personal data do not restrict Corvex's use of aggregated or de-identified data that no longer identifies a specific individual, which Corvex may use for product improvement, benchmarking, or similar purposes.

### 6.3 Enterprise Order Form Variations

Enterprise customers may negotiate specific data processing terms, including a Data Processing Agreement, as part of their signed Order Form. Where such negotiated terms differ from this policy's default provisions, the Order Form and any associated Data Processing Agreement take precedence for that customer's account.

### 6.4 Jurisdiction-Specific Requirements

Where applicable law in a specific jurisdiction imposes requirements different from the general provisions of this policy, Corvex applies the jurisdiction-specific requirement for the individuals and data to which it applies, consistent with Section 7.

## 7. Compliance

### 7.1 Regulatory Alignment

Corvex designs its data handling practices with reference to widely recognized data protection principles, including purpose limitation, data minimization, and security safeguards. Because Corvex serves customers across multiple jurisdictions, specific compliance obligations may vary by region; Section 6.4 addresses how jurisdiction-specific requirements are incorporated.

### 7.2 Data Processing Agreements

Corvex offers a Data Processing Agreement to customers who require one to satisfy their own regulatory obligations regarding End User data, available through the customer's Customer Success Manager or the Corvex Legal & Compliance team.

### 7.3 Sub-processor Oversight

Corvex maintains a current list of Sub-processors engaged in processing personal data, available to customers upon request, and reviews Sub-processor data protection practices as part of ongoing vendor risk management led by Security & Trust.

### 7.4 Internal Audit

Corvex's data handling practices are subject to periodic internal review by Legal & Compliance and Security & Trust to confirm ongoing alignment with this policy and applicable law.

### 7.5 Policy Review Cadence

This policy is reviewed at least annually, and additionally whenever a material change to Corvex Cloud's data processing practices, infrastructure, or the applicable regulatory environment warrants earlier review.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Corvex sell my personal data?**
No. Corvex does not sell personal data to third parties, consistent with the Corvex Technologies Company Overview.

**Who owns the data my customers submit through CloudDesk Chat or CloudDesk Tickets?**
As the customer organization using Corvex Cloud, you are generally the Controller of your End Users' data. Corvex processes that data as your Processor, according to your instructions and configuration.

**How do I request a copy of, or the deletion of, my personal data?**
If you are an End User of a Corvex customer's support operation, contact that customer directly, since they are generally the Controller of your data. If you are a Corvex Cloud Customer User or a website visitor, contact Corvex directly using the information in Section 10.

**Does Corvex use my data to train AI models used by other customers?**
Corvex's AI-assisted features, available on Enterprise plans, are designed to operate within the context of a single customer's own account and data. Corvex does not use one customer's data to improve or train features delivered to a different, unrelated customer.

**Where is my data physically stored?**
By default, customer data is hosted within Corvex's standard, primary infrastructure regions. Enterprise customers with specific regional requirements can request a data residency configuration through their Customer Success Manager.

**Does Corvex offer a Data Processing Agreement?**
Yes. Customers who require a Data Processing Agreement to satisfy their own regulatory obligations can request one through their Customer Success Manager or Corvex Legal & Compliance.

**What happens to my data if I cancel my Corvex Cloud subscription?**
Your data is retained in an inactive, exportable state for 30 days following cancellation, after which it is permanently deleted, consistent with the Corvex Cloud Pricing Guide and Refund Policy.

**Does Corvex share my data with the integrations I connect?**
Data is shared with an integration only according to the specific configuration you set up under Admin Dashboard > Integrations; Corvex does not independently share your data with an integration you have not configured.

**How does Corvex secure my data?**
Corvex encrypts data in transit and at rest, applies role-based access controls, and offers additional security capabilities such as SSO and IP allowlisting depending on your plan, as described in the relevant Administrator Guide.

**Who can I contact with privacy questions or concerns?**
See Section 10 for Corvex's privacy contact information.
