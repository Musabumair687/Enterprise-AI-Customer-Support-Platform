## Category: Company

### 1. Question: What is Corvex Technologies?

**Answer:** Corvex Technologies, Inc. is the company behind Corvex Cloud, a customer experience and support automation platform. Founded in 2015 and headquartered in Austin, Texas, Corvex builds cloud-based software that helps support teams manage conversations, tickets, and reporting in one connected system.
